When measurement goes wrong

Both halves were built — to two different measurements.

Physics begins where guessing ends.

Measure first. Build second.

A staircase that ends in the ceiling

The height was never compared with a standard.

A drain above the water it should drain

A few centimetres decide whether the road floods.

Every one of these is a number without a proper unit behind it.
