Parts of the Eye — and what each one does

Cornea

Transparent front part. Refracts light — 2/3 of the eye's total optical power.

Iris

Annular structure in the eye. Controls the diameter and size of the pupil, and so controls the amount of light reaching the retina.

Pupil

Opening in the centre. Allows light to enter the eye.

Retina

Innermost, light-sensitive layer. Translates the image into electrical neural impulses to the brain.

Ciliary muscle

Controls accommodation for viewing objects at varying distances. It does this by changing the shape of the lens.

Rod cells

Responsible for vision in low light conditions.

Cone cells

Responsible for vision in bright light, and for colour vision.

Hold on to this

The cornea does most of the bending; the ciliary muscle and lens only fine-tune it. That is why accommodation has a limited range.
