Supplementary units (Plane angle)

Plane angle : the opening an arc makes at the centre of its circle.
θ = arcr

Both are lengths, so the ratio carries no dimension — an angle is a pure number.

Unit: radian (rad) One radian is the angle whose arc is exactly as long as the radius.
Whole circle arc = 2πr, so θ = 2π rad = 360°  →  1 rad ≈ 57.3°
θ is the arc divided by the radius
