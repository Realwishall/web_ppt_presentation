Solid angle

Solid angle : the opening a surface makes at a point — a cone in space, not a wedge in a plane.
Ω = Sr2

Area divided by area — dimensionless again. Its unit is the steradian (sr).

Question: Find solid angle for full sphere.
Solution: the surface that surrounds the point completely is the whole sphere, S = 4πr².
Ω = 4πr² ÷ r² = 4π steradian ≈ 12.57 sr
Compare a full turn in a plane is 2π rad; a full look-around in space is 4π sr.
The cap of area S at distance r subtends the solid angle Ω
