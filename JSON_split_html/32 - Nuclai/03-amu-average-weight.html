What is 1 amu?

The ruler we use to weigh atoms.

What is 1 amu

It is defined as one-twelfth the mass of a carbon-12 atom.

1 amu= Mass of C1212
mp= 1.00727 u
mn= 1.00866 u
mn>mp  — the neutron is slightly heavier
1 u ≈ 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg — worth writing in the margin

Average Atomic Weight of Isotopes

Average atomic weight of isotope

Here we take a weighted average — each isotope contributes by its abundance.

wavg= w1x1+ w2x2+ w3x3… 100

w = isotope weight, x = its percentage in nature.

Same maths as your marks-weightage — nothing new
Question
Find the average atomic weight of X if it exists in three forms — 22.06, 22.94 and 23.06 — with percentages 22%, 40%, 38%.
Solution
Wavg= 22.06×22+22.94×40+23.06×38100
or, with base 22.06:  0×22+0.88×40+1×38100 = 0+35.2+38100 =0.732
Wavg=22.06+0.732= 22.792
The base trick kills the heavy multiplication

Isotopes — good to know

  • In Greek, isotope means "the same place" — they occupy the same box in the periodic table.
  • Practically every element is a mixture of several isotopes.
  • The two heavier isotopes of hydrogen are deuterium and tritium. Tritium nuclei are unstable — they don't occur naturally and are made in laboratories.
  • The presence of isotopes gave a hint of the existence of the neutron.
