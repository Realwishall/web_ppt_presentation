Bigger A, bigger nucleus — but how exactly?

Size of nucleus

# For radius

R= RoA1/3
R=1.2×10−15⁢A1/3 m
R1R2= (A1A2)1/3

# For density

ρ=massvol ∝AR3
∝A(A1/3)3=AA
=Constant ≈2.3×1017 kg/m³

Density is independent of A — every nucleus is packed equally tight.

R ∝ A^⅓ ⇒ volume ∝ A — nucleons behave like closely packed marbles
