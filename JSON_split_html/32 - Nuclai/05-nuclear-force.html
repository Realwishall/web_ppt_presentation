Nuclear Force

Something must beat proton–proton repulsion — meet the strongest force in nature.

Nuclear force

  • Range of nuclear force is small
  • Range of strong nuclear force is 10−15 m
  • Range of weak nuclear force is 10−16 m
  • In general nuclear force is attractive in nature, but if distance is < 0.8 fermi it becomes repulsive
  • Nuclear force is charge independent, i.e. nuclear force between Fpp=Fpn=Fnn

Nuclear force — character

  • Nuclear force does not follow superposition — superposition is not a property of this force
  • Force is Non-Conservative in nature  (conservative / non-conservative)
  • Does not follow the inverse square law
  • Force is Non-central  (central / non-central)
