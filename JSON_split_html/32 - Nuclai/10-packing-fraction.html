NOTE:

Standard reference values used in binding-energy problems.

Binding energy

of hydrogen atom / single proton / single neutron

= 0

Mass

of hydrogen atom / single proton / single neutron

= 1 amu

A lone nucleon has nothing to bind to — so its binding energy is zero and its mass is taken as 1 amu.

Packing fraction

Packing fraction =  M-AA

M = actual atomic mass  ·  A = mass number

  • Zero for C-12 (the amu is defined from it)
  • Can be positive, negative, or zero
  • Smaller packing fraction → more stable
Lower packing fraction means the nucleus is more tightly packed and stable
