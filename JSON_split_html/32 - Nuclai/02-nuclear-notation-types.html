Nuclei · Part 1

Nuclear Notation

Two numbers tell you everything about a nucleus.

Nuclear notation

XZA
A — Atomic Mass number: total number of protons (Z or P) and neutrons (N) — together called nucleons
Z — Atomic Number: total number of protons
N=A−Z  (number of neutrons)

Atomic mass is just the mass of the atom.

A on top, Z at the bottom — protons decide the element
