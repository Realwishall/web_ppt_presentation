Question on Energy

Mass that disappears in a reaction comes out as energy — let's compute how much.

Finding energy emitted in a reaction

Example 1 · in joules

A+B→C
ma=4g mb=5g mc=7g
ΔM= 2 g
E=ΔMc2
=2×10-3×(3×108)2
= 18×1013 J

Example 2 · in amu

A+B→C
ma=3amu mb=5amu mc=6amu
ΔM= 2 amu
E=2×931 MeV
= 1862 MeV
1 amu ↔ 931 MeV is the shortcut when masses are in amu

Binding energy → Total energy

Energy released in a reaction = binding energy of products − binding energy of reactants.

A+B→C
B.E. of A = 13 MeV
B.E. of B = 18 MeV
B.E. of C = 26 MeV
Released = BEC-(BEA+BEB)
=26-(13+18)
= -5 MeV
NOTE:

Negative → this reaction absorbs energy, so it is not possible on its own.

Which ball has greater binding energy?

More binding energy → lower total energy → more stable.

Deeper in the well = more bound = more stable, even though it holds less energy

What every reaction is doing

This reaction is Not Possible
