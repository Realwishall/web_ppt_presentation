Find energy emitted · Q1
Question
A+B→C
Binding energy of A =13 MeV Binding energy of B =11 MeV Binding energy of C =26 MeV
Solution
Energy =  BEC-(BEA+BEB)
=26-(13+11)
= 2 MeV  released → reaction possible
Products more tightly bound than reactants → energy comes out
Find energy emitted · Q2
Question
X25+ Y38→ Z513
B.E. per nucleon of X =7.2 MeV B.E. per nucleon of Y =7.5 MeV B.E. per nucleon of Z =8 MeV
Solution
=13×8-(8×7.5+5×7.2)
=104-96= 8 MeV

Or with reference 7.2 MeV

=13×0.8-(8×0.3+5×0)
=10.4-2.4= 8 MeV

Subtract 7.2 from each per-nucleon value — same answer, smaller numbers.

Solution
=2×(6×8.5)-(8×7.5+5×7.2)

Find energy emitted · Q4
Question
X25+ Y38→ 2Z26+p
mass of X =5.5 amu mass of Y =9 amu mass of Z atom =6.5 amu
Solution
E =  (mreact-mprod)×931
=(5.5+9-13-1)×931
=0.5×931= 465.5 MeV
products = 2×6.5 + 1 = 13 + 1 amu
