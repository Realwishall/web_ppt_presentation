Mass Defect

The whole weighs less than the sum of its parts.

Mass Defect

Mass defect is the difference between the actual atomic mass and the predicted mass calculated by adding the mass of protons and neutrons present in the nucleus.

For XZA: Expected mass =  Zmp+(A−Z)mn
Real mass =  mx < Expected mass
ΔM= (Expected mass)−(Real mass)
ΔM=Zmp+(A−Z)mn−mx
The missing mass didn't vanish — it left as energy

Binding Energy

Binding energy

Mass defect is lost mass — energy used to bind the protons and neutrons inside the nucleus.

or say
It is the amount of energy required to break this element into separate protons and neutrons.

And this energy is called Binding energy.

Binding energy = ΔMc2
BE per nucleon = ΔMc2A
Higher BE per nucleon → more stable nucleus (coming up)
Question
Which of the following reactions is possible in nature?
n01→ p11+ e−10   or   p11→ n01+ e10
Solution
mn>mp
n→p — possible in nature (heavier → lighter, energy released)
p→n — not possible in nature outside the nucleus
NOTE:

This is why a free neutron is not stable.
