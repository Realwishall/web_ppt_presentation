Alternating Current · Part 7

AC Across a Resistor

The simplest case — no phase shift, no frequency dependence.

AC supply along R

  • Apply v=V0sinωt across a resistor R
  • By Ohm's law: i=vR=V0Rsinωt=i0sinωt
  • Current has the same phase as voltage — V and I are in phase
  • Resistance R does not depend on frequency — it's the same for AC and DC
v and i cross zero together and peak together — no phase lag or lead

Phasor diagram for R

Both phasors point along the same line — zero phase angle between V and I.
