Alternating Current · Part 9

AC Across a Capacitor

The mirror image of the inductor — current now runs ahead of voltage.

AC supply along C

  • Apply v=V0sinωt across a capacitor: charge q=Cv=CV0sinωt
  • Current i=dqdt gives i=V01/ωCsin(ωt+π2)
  • Current leads the voltage by π/2 (90°)
  • Define capacitive reactance XC=1/ωC — "something like resistance" for a capacitor
  • XC decreases with frequency — at DC (f=0), XC→∞: a capacitor is a perfect insulator for DC (blocks it completely)
