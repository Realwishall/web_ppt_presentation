Resonance

The one special frequency where the circuit stops "fighting" the supply at all.

Inductive, capacitive, or resistive?

ConditionCircuit behaves asPhase
XL>XCInductiveV leads I
XL<XCCapacitiveI leads V
XL=XCPurely resistive — resonanceI, V in phase

The resonance condition

  • At resonance, XL=XC, so the reactive part of impedance cancels: Z=R — its minimum possible value
  • Since I=V/Z, current is maximum at resonance
  • Resonant angular frequency: ω0=1LC — same formula as free LC oscillation
  • A series resonant circuit is called an acceptor circuit — it offers minimum impedance (accepts current) at its resonant frequency, e.g. tuning in one radio station from many

Current vs frequency
