Free fall

Motion under gravity alone — one acceleration, every body

Special points on Free fall

  • In free fall acceleration is constant even if mass of body is different
  • Can use all kinematics equation
  • Can use all speed equation
  • Can use ratio technique
Air Friction We assume no air friction
Dropping velocity
Dropping velocity is not zero but same as source
Dropping acceleration is simply g in downward direction

Can use all kinematics equation

⇒ v = u + at
⇒ s = ut + 12at2
⇒ v2 = u2 + 2a·s
⇒ snth = u + a2(2n − 1)
⇒ vavg = u + v2
⇒ s = u + v2 t
⇒ vat d/2 = √u2 + v22

Can use all speed equation

If u or v = 0
⇒ v = at
⇒ s = 12 at2
⇒ v = √2as
⇒ t = va
⇒ t = √2sa
⇒ s = v2/2a
