Question
Find acceleration and tension in given case, with m1>m3m_1>m_3
Solution
Only the slope component of m3m_3's weight fights m1gm_1g.
⇒a=(m1−m3sin⁡θ)gm1+m3\Rightarrow a=\frac{(m_1-m_3\sin\theta)g}{m_1+m_3}
Ring m3m_3 alone: T drags it up the slope against m3gsin⁡θm_3g\sin\theta.
⇒T=m3a+m3gsin⁡θ\Rightarrow T=m_3a+m_3g\sin\theta
Question
Find acceleration and tension in given case — the same wedge with m2m_2 lying on its flat top, m1>m3m_1>m_3
Solution
⇒a=(m1−m3sin⁡θ)gm1+m2+m3\Rightarrow a=\frac{(m_1-m_3\sin\theta)g}{m_1+m_2+m_3}
⇒T2=m3a+m3gsin⁡θ\Rightarrow T_2=m_3a+m_3g\sin\theta
Ring m2m_2 and m3m_3 together for the other string.
⇒T1=(m2+m3)a+m3gsin⁡θ\Rightarrow T_1=(m_2+m_3)a+m_3g\sin\theta

Question
Find acceleration and tension in given case, with m1>m2+m3m_1>m_2+m_3
Solution
Three weights on one string: m1gm_1g drives, m2gsin⁡θm_2g\sin\theta and m3gm_3g resist.
⇒a=(m1−m2sin⁡θ−m3)gm1+m2+m3\Rightarrow a=\frac{(m_1-m_2\sin\theta-m_3)g}{m_1+m_2+m_3}
⇒T2=m3a+m3g\Rightarrow T_2=m_3a+m_3g
⇒T1=(m2+m3)a+m3g+m2gsin⁡θ\Rightarrow T_1=(m_2+m_3)a+m_3g+m_2g\sin\theta
ORT1=m1(g−a)OR\quad T_1=m_1(g-a)quicker check
Question
Find acceleration and tension — M1M_1 and M2M_2 joined over a pulley at the apex of a double wedge, base angles θ\theta and ϕ\phi
Solution
Each block only feels the slope part of its own weight.
Driving force =M2gsin⁡θ−M1gsin⁡ϕ=M_2g\sin\theta-M_1g\sin\phi
⇒a=(M2sin⁡θ−M1sin⁡ϕ)gM1+M2\Rightarrow a=\frac{(M_2\sin\theta-M_1\sin\phi)g}{M_1+M_2}
Ring the block that goes up.
⇒T=M1a+M1gsin⁡ϕ\Rightarrow T=M_1a+M_1g\sin\phi

Question
Find acceleration and tension in given case — F pushes m1m_1, which pushes m2m_2 in front of it
Solution
They touch but are not tied — the internal force is a normal reaction.
⇒a=Fm1+m2\Rightarrow a=\frac{F}{m_1+m_2}
Ring m2m_2: the only push on it is N.
⇒N=m2a\Rightarrow N=m_2a

Question
Find acceleration and tension in given case — 3F pushes right on m1m_1 while F pushes left on m3m_3
Solution
Net outside push is 3F−F=2F3F-F=2F.
⇒a=2Fm1+m2+m3\Rightarrow a=\frac{2F}{m_1+m_2+m_3}
Ring m2,m3m_2,m_3: now F also crosses that ring.
⇒N1=(m2+m3)a+F\Rightarrow N_1=(m_2+m_3)a+F
⇒N2=(m3)a+F\Rightarrow N_2=(m_3)a+F

Question
Find Tension at distance x — a uniform rod of length L and mass m is squeezed: 20 N pushes in from the left, 30 N pushes in from the right
Solution
Both pushes point inward, so the rod is in compression and the net force is their difference.
a=30−20ma=\frac{30-20}{m}directed left
Now cut at x and ring the left piece, of mass mLx\frac{m}{L}x.
20−N=(mLx)a20-N=\left(\tfrac{m}{L}x\right)a
Question
Find Tension at distance x — the same rod stretched: 20 N pulls out to the left, 30 N pulls out to the right
Solution
Both pulls point outward — the rod is in tension, but the net force is still the difference.
a=30−20ma=\frac{30-20}{m}directed right
Ring the left piece again; here the internal force pulls it to the right.
T−20=(mLx)aT-20=\left(\tfrac{m}{L}x\right)a
Question
Find Tension at distance x — now both forces act the same way: 20 N pushes from the left and 30 N pulls from the right
Solution
Same direction, so this time they add.
a=30+20ma=\frac{30+20}{m}
The sign in the numerator is the whole question — read the arrows before you read the numbers.

Question
A block m on a smooth wedge of angle θ\theta is held by a string that runs over a small pulley to the wedge's back wall; the string leaves the block at angle θ\theta to the slope. Find the tension and the normal reaction.
Solution
Take the axes along and perpendicular to the slope — not horizontal and vertical.
Along the slope: Fcos⁡θ=mgsin⁡θF\cos\theta=mg\sin\theta
Perpendicular: N+Fsin⁡θ=mgcos⁡θN+F\sin\theta=mg\cos\theta
⇒F=mgtan⁡θ\Rightarrow F=mg\tan\theta
⇒N=mgcos⁡θ−mgtan⁡θsin⁡θ\Rightarrow N=mg\cos\theta-mg\tan\theta\sin\theta
