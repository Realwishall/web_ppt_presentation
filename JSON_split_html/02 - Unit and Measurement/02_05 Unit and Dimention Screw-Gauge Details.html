Unit and Measurement

Screw Gauge

Pitch

The distance the spindle advances along its axis in one complete turn of the thimble.

Least count

1 turn corresponds to Pitch n divisions on circular dial correspond to Pitch
1 div = Least Count =PitchNumber of divisions on circular dial

Reading

Reading = Main scale reading + (Least count × Circular scale reading)

Final Answer

Final Reading = Reading − Error

Zero Error

No Error

Zero error = 0

Positive Error

Zero error = +9 × L.C.

Negative Error

Zero error = −(100 − 89) × L.C.
= −11 × L.C.
