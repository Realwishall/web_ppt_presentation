Screw gauge

what it can measure — and what it cannot

Screw gauge · key points

Least count Least count of a screw gauge is 10−5 m  ( = 0.01 mm = 0.001 cm )
What can it measure? It can be used to measure things that can be fitted inside the jaws.
Like
  • Thickness of a coin
  • Diameter of a sphere
  • Diameter of a thin wire

Question

Ask yourself Can we use a screw gauge to measure …
Inner diameter of a ring
Depth of a cylinder
Thickness of a coin
Why A screw gauge has only one kind of jaw — a flat anvil and a flat spindle facing each other.

Anything you cannot place between those two faces cannot be measured.
So No inside jaws → no inner diameter.
No depth stem → no depth.
