Where does a vernier come from?

QuestionWhat is the length of the pencil?
Answer length = a − b
QuestionWhat is the length of the smallest gap you can read?
Answer smallest gap = a − b  — one main division minus one vernier division
That is the whole idea Two slightly different divisions, subtracted, give a gap far smaller than either scale alone. That tiny gap is the least count.

Vernier caliper · key points

Least count Least count of a vernier caliper is 10−4 m  ( = 0.1 mm = 0.01 cm )
What can it measure? Anything that can be fitted inside the inner jaws, held in the outer jaws, or reached by the depth stem.
Like
  • Depth of a cylinder — depth stem
  • Inner diameter of a cylinder — upper jaws
  • Outer diameter of a cylinder — lower jaws

Least count of a vernier

Below the line — one

Question with zero error · read the vernier zero

No error
vernier 0 sits exactly on main 0
+ Ve error
vernier 0 is to the right of main 0
− Ve error
vernier 0 is to the left of main 0
For − ve error Main scale reading will be − 1 MSD  — the vernier zero has not yet reached the main zero.

NOTE · reading the main scale

For a complex question, take all measurements in mm.
If the question says the rod is between 4.2 cm and 4.3 cm, the main scale reading is 42 mm
If the question says the rod is between 64 mm and 64.5 mm, the main scale reading is 64 mm and 1 MSD is 0.5 mm
If the vernier zero is on the right side of 26 mm on the main scale, the main scale reading is 26 mm
If the vernier zero is on the left side of 26 mm on the main scale, the main scale reading is 25 mm
Rule of thumb Always take the main scale mark just before the vernier zero — never the one after it.
