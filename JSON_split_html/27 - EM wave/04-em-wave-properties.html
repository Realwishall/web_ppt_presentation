Maxwell Wave Theory

Changing E→ makes B→, changing B→ makes E→ — together they fly.

Maxwell wave theory

E→= Eo⁢sin (ωt−kx) j^
B→= Bo⁢sin (ωt−kx) k^
⟹ an EM wave travelling along +x

Both fields oscillate in phase, in mutually perpendicular planes, feeding each other as they travel.

All basic properties of sin

Direction and phase

  • Direction of EM wave  =  along E→×B→
  • E and B phase difference  =  0  →  same phase
  • Direction of E→ and B→  = perpendicular planes
Peaks together, zeros together — but at right angles
