Maxwell Equations

∮ E→· ds→ = qinεo
→ Gauss's law in electrostatics
Electric flux through a closed surface counts the charge inside.
∮ B→· ds→ = 0
→ Gauss's law in magnetism
No magnetic monopoles — field lines always close on themselves.
∮ E→· dl→ = − dφBdt
→ Faraday's law
A changing magnetic flux creates a (rotational) electric field.
∮ B→· dl→ = μo [Iin+Id]
→ Ampère–Maxwell law
Currents and changing electric flux (Id=εodφedt) create magnetic fields.
Last two together: changing B ↔ changing E — the seed of the EM wave
