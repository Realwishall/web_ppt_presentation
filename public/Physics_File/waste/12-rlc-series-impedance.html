Alternating Current · Part 12

RLC Series Circuit

Three elements, three phase relationships — add their voltages like vectors, not numbers.

Treat voltages as vectors

  • Same current I flows through R, L, C (series) — use it as the reference
  • VR is in phase with I
  • VL leads I by 90°
  • VC lags I by 90° — so VL and VC point in opposite directions
  • Resultant supply voltage: V=VR2+(VL−VC)2

Impedance triangle

  • Dividing every voltage vector by the common current I gives the same triangle in terms of resistance/reactance
  • Z=R2+(XL−XC)2 — the net impedance of the circuit
  • Phase angle: tanφ=XL−XCR
  • Current: I=V/Z
Practise writing the voltage equation, impedance equation, and current form for R, L, C, and net — all in one line each
