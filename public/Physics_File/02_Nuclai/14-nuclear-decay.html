NCERT Extra Point · 1
Common statement

"Mass–energy interconversion takes place only in nuclear reactions and never in chemical reactions."

Strictly speaking, this is incorrect.

Reason

Energy and mass represent the same physical quantity — so mass changes in chemical reactions too, just far too tiny to notice.

NCERT Extra Point · 2
Observation

The binding energy per nucleon curve is smooth and slowly varying over most of the range.

Reason

The nuclear force is short range, so each added nucleon interacts with roughly the same number of neighbours — the binding per nucleon stays nearly constant.

NCERT Extra Point · 3
Observation

Binding energy shows peaks at nuclides like He24, O816, etc.

Reason

These peaks are taken as evidence of an atom-like shell structure inside the nucleus — certain nucleon counts form especially stable, "closed" shells.
