Click-to-answer questions

Three question types you can tap directly on the board: single correct, multiple correct, and numerical.

Load this file with the Master Library button, then tap options straight on the slide — the ink layer lets these clickable answers through.

Single correct Q1

A body is thrown vertically up. At the highest point of its motion, which quantity is zero?

Explanation: at the top, speed is momentarily 0, but gravity (and hence acceleration ≈ 9.8 m/s²) still acts. So velocity is zero.

Numerical Q3

A car accelerates uniformly from rest at 4 m/s² for 5 s. What is its final speed (in m/s)?

0
